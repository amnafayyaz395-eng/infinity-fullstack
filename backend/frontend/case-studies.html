Telecom

New customer acquisition across three UK regions

National Telecom Provider

A national telecom brand needed to grow subscriber numbers in three regions ahead of a product launch, without relying solely on paid digital channels.

Infinity Marketing fielded a trained acquisition team across London, Manchester and Leeds, running door and retail-adjacent campaigns with real-time qualification. Weekly reporting let the client reallocate reps to the highest-converting territories mid-campaign.

Retail

In-store brand awareness and footfall campaign

High-Street Retail Chain

A retail chain wanted to rebuild footfall and brand recall in flagship locations following a rebrand.

Brand ambassadors were placed at flagship stores to introduce the rebrand directly to passers-by, backed by a simple, consistent message across every location. Daily reporting tracked footfall against a pre-campaign baseline.

Energy

Lead generation for a new tariff offering

UK Energy Supplier

An energy supplier needed qualified leads for a new tariff, in a sector where trust is the biggest barrier to conversion.

Reps were trained specifically on tariff detail and common objections, so every lead handed to the client's sales team had already had its key questions answered — sharply reducing drop-off later in the funnel.
