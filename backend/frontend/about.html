About Infinity Marketing

Built by people who've carried a sales target themselves.

Infinity Marketing & Advertisement was founded in London in 2015 on a simple belief: a well-trained person, in the right conversation, will always outperform an anonymous ad.

Our Story

From one campaign to a team the corporate market trusts

Infinity Marketing started as a small outsourced sales operation running door and event campaigns for a handful of London brands. What set the early team apart wasn't budget — it was discipline: every rep trained properly, every campaign measured, every client given a straight answer on what was and wasn't working.

That approach is the same one we run today, just at a larger scale. Our leadership brings more than 26 years of combined experience in direct sales and field marketing, and it shows in how tightly every campaign is planned, staffed and reported.

Mission & Values

What we're actually trying to do

Deliver cost-effective, high-performance marketing that increases sales, generates new business and builds brand awareness — for every client, not just the largest ones.

Tailored over templated

Every campaign is built for the client in front of us, not pulled off a shelf.

Accountable to the number

We report performance honestly, including when a campaign needs to change course.

People-first delivery

Well-trained, well-managed reps are the whole product — we invest accordingly.
